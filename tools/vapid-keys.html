Push key generator

Creates the pair of keys SqueveTrack's background reminders need. Everything happens inside this page on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Open this file from your computer or phone — do not upload it to your website. Treat the private key like a password.

1. Public key

Goes in push-config.json and in the Supabase secret VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY.

2. Private key

Goes only in the Supabase secret VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY. Never put it in the app or GitHub.
